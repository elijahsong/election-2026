
| Dataset | Source | Years | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| House election results | [MIT Election Lab](https://electionlab.mit.edu/data) | 2008–2024 | Dependent variable: Democratic two-party House margin | Essential |
| Presidential results by congressional district | [The Downballot](https://www.the-downballot.com/p/data) | Historical presidential elections on corresponding district boundaries | Calculate district partisan lean relative to national presidential margin | Essential |
| Historical national generic ballot | [FiveThirtyEight](https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/data/tree/master/congress-generic-ballot) | 2008–2024 | Historical national political environment; final pre-election polling averages | Essential |
| 2026 national generic ballot | [FiftyPlusOne (50+1)](https://fiftyplusone.news/) | 2026 | Current national political environment | Essential |
| Incumbency and open-seat records | [The Downballot](https://www.the-downballot.com/p/data) / [MIT Election Lab](https://electionlab.mit.edu/data) | 2008–2026 | Identify Democratic incumbents, Republican incumbents, and open seats | Essential |
| SHAVE-adjusted national House vote | [Split Ticket](https://split-ticket.org/data-repository/) | 2008–2024 | National House vote adjusted for uncontested elections; generic-ballot validation | Secondary |
| District college-educated share | [U.S. Census ACS](https://data.census.gov/) | Historical and current ACS vintages | Optional demographic predictor: share of adults 25+ with a bachelor's degree or higher | Optional |
| District Hispanic share | [U.S. Census CVAP](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/voting-rights/cvap.html) | Historical and current vintages | Optional demographic predictor: Hispanic population or citizen voting-age population share | Optional |
| 2026 candidates and election rules | [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026) / state election authorities | 2026 | Identify uncontested, same-party top-two, RCV, and other exceptional elections | Essential |
